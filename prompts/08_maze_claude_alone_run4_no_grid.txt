You are a maze solver. You will be given a 30 x 30 maze as the list of its free cells. Rows are numbered 0 to 29 from top to bottom and columns 0 to 29 from left to right; a cell is written (row, column). Every cell that is not in the list is a wall (impassable). You will also be given the start cell S and the goal cell G, which are free cells.
Find a path of orthogonally adjacent (up/down/left/right) free cells that connects S to G without passing through any wall. Diagonal moves are not allowed. Respond with ONLY the path as a list of the (row, column) coordinates of the cells it visits, in order from S to G, including S and G, in the form [(r, c), (r, c), ...]. Do not include prose, code fences, or any other text. Example response format (a path of 8 cells in a 6 x 6 maze; your path must go from S to G of your maze): [(1, 1), (1, 2), (1, 3), (1, 4), (2, 4), (3, 4), (4, 4), (4, 5)]

Method: use breadth-first search. Start at G and spread out one step at a time. The free cells next to G are 1 step away. The free cells next to those that you have not reached yet are 2 steps away.
